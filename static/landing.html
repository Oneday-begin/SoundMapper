GOOD TO KNOW

Before you press play.

Do I need to understand music?

No. Start with two songs you know. Compare their characteristics, then play the suggested passages. Detailed settings are optional.

What do I need to get started?

Two recordings from your library, or two MP3 or WAV files you have permission to use. A new upload may need a little time to analyze; the app shows its progress.

Where does my audio go?

Your uploaded recordings and their analysis are stored locally on this computer. The app does not send your songs to a paid cloud service. Initial setup may download software and models.

What does a similarity score mean?

It summarizes particular audio characteristics. It is a starting point for exploration, not a percentage of identical music or proof of copying. Some results are estimates, and the app shows when evidence is unavailable.
